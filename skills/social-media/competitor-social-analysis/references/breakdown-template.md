# Creative breakdown template

Fill one per-post card for each top performer, then — the real deliverable —
the cross-post synthesis at the bottom. Mark anything you could not verify as
`unverified:` rather than guessing silently. Metadata-only passes (no video
watched) can fill Hook hypothesis, Packaging, and Cadence; everything else
needs the video or founder screenshots.

## Per-post card

```markdown
### <rank>. <title> — <url>
- Performance: <views> views | ER <engagement_rate> | <views_vs_median>x account median
- Format: <duration> | <vertical/horizontal> | <talking head / screen capture / montage / animation>

**Hook (first 1–3 s):** What stops the scroll? (on-screen text, spoken line,
visual surprise, result-first reveal). Quote the actual hook text/line.
**Structure & pacing:** Beat map (hook → setup → payoff → CTA). Average shot
length; where the video would lose you.
**Editing style:** Cut rhythm, zooms/punch-ins, transitions, B-roll density,
screen-capture vs filmed, meme inserts.
**Fonts & captions:** Font family (or closest match), weight, case, color,
stroke/shadow, placement, animation (word-by-word pop, karaoke), caption
coverage (full transcript vs highlights).
**Voiceover:** Human or AI (which voice, if identifiable), tone, pace,
scripted vs improvised feel.
**Color grade:** Natural / punchy / teal-orange / desaturated / high-key;
consistency with their other posts.
**Sound:** Trending audio vs original; music genre/energy; SFX usage.
**CTA:** What is asked, when (early/end), on-screen vs spoken vs caption.
```

## Cross-post synthesis (the deliverable)

```markdown
## What the winners have in common
- **Hooks:** <the 2–3 hook formulas that repeat across top performers>
- **Format:** <duration band, orientation, content type that wins>
- **Editing signature:** <the recognizable style: pacing, cuts, captions>
- **Voice & tone:** <VO style, energy, person (I/you/we)>
- **Look:** <fonts, grade, framing that make their content recognizable>
- **What their flops lack:** <compare bottom-quartile posts — which of the
  above elements are missing or weaker there>
- **Cadence:** <posts/week, and whether winners cluster around launches>
```

The flops comparison matters: a pattern present in winners AND flops is brand
style, not a performance driver. Only patterns that separate the quartiles
should drive content ideas.
